Routel is a freight forwarding platform. It runs the full life of a shipment, from the first quotation to the last payment, and keeps your books current from the same data. It grew out of the internal system a working freight forwarder in Malaysia uses every day, and it is now offered to other forwarders as a hosted service.

## Why Routel

Most forwarders run on a patchwork: a spreadsheet of rates, a template for quotations, another for the Bill of Lading, an accounting package that someone updates at month end, and a shared drive of PDFs. Every hand-off between those tools is a place where a number gets re-typed or a document gets lost.

Routel keeps one record of the shipment, the booking, and lets every department work on it:

- **Pricing** maintains vendor buying rates by route and container type, so quotes are never built on an expired price.
- **Sales** turns a current rate into a quotation for the customer.
- **Operations** works the booking created from that quotation: confirms space with the carrier, tracks containers and dates, and generates the House Bill of Lading.
- **Accounting** invoices the customer from the booking's charges, records vendor costs and overheads, and reads reports that are computed live from those entries.
- **Management** sees profitability per booking, per customer, per branch and for the company.

Because everything posts to one General Ledger as it happens, there are no manual journal entries and no second set of numbers to reconcile.

## How a booking flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quote">
    Sales picks a buying rate for the route and container type and issues a quotation to the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Booking">
    The accepted quotation is converted into a booking. The route, equipment and the chosen route's charges carry over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and ship">
    Operations records the carrier booking, issues the Booking Confirmation, keeps the shipping timeline current and attaches documents as they arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bill of Lading">
    The House B/L and its container annexure are generated from the booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoice and cost">
    Invoices are created from the booking's sale (AR) lines in your base currency or USD; vendor costs sit on the same booking as cost (AP) lines, converted at the recorded exchange rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report">
    Gross profit, net income, aging and cash position update themselves from the entries above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Currencies

Invoices and books run in exactly two currencies: your **base currency** (MYR in Malaysia, IDR in Indonesia) and **USD**. Costs recorded in a different currency are converted with the exchange rate captured on the line, so booking profitability compares like with like. The **Group** view puts every branch side by side, consolidated in USD.
