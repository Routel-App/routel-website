Routel runs a freight forwarder's whole business on one record per shipment, the booking: rates, quotations, carrier bookings, the Bill of Lading, invoices, vendor costs, payments and the ledger. It grew out of the internal system a working forwarder uses every day, and it is now offered to other forwarders as a hosted service.

At its core is an **AI assistant that operates the system**. It does not just answer questions about your data. It calls the system's own actions to search, report, read documents and prepare real work, and every change it prepares waits for a person to confirm it.

## How the assistant works

The assistant is a layer over the same actions the screens use, not a separate system with its own access to the database. That is what makes it safe to use on live operations and live accounts.

<AssistantFlow />

- **The same actions, the same rules.** Each tool is one of Routel's own operations: create a booking, finalise an invoice, record a payment, run the P&L. On confirmation the server validates the values and checks the user's role again, exactly as it does for a form.
- **A proposal, never a silent write.** Anything that writes stops as a card showing every field, line and amount. The user confirms, edits or cancels; there is no setting that skips this.
- **Safe to retry.** Each proposal has its own identity. A second click returns the first result instead of writing twice, and a confirmation sent while the first is still running is refused.
- **Accountable.** Every confirmed action is logged with the person who confirmed it, their role, the action, its values and the outcome. Notes, tasks, follow-ups, filed e-mails and draft quotations can be undone for ten minutes; anything that moved money is reversed with its own document.
- **Hard to steer from outside.** It won't store passwords, keys or card numbers in its memory, and it stops saving memory once a conversation has read outside material such as an uploaded file, an e-mail or a public form.
- **Private by design.** Requests to the AI model are sent with storage turned off at the provider. Conversation history stays in Routel, private to each user.

The full guide, with everything it reads and prepares: [AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).

## Built for real operations

- **One record per shipment.** Pricing keeps buying rates by lane and container with validity, Sales quotes from a current rate, Operations works the booking that quotation became, Accounting invoices from the booking's own charges, and Management sees profit per booking, customer and branch. Nothing is re-typed between departments.
- **Books that keep themselves.** Invoices, payments, notes, vendor costs and expenses post to one General Ledger as they happen. Debits equal credits by construction, there are no manual journal entries, and a finalised invoice is locked.
- **Controls where they matter.** An invoice can't be finalised without the carrier's Master B/L number, and a customer's current credit exposure is shown against their limit when a booking is created for them.
- **Its own server and database per company.** Each company, or each legal entity in a group, runs on its own deployment with its own database, base currency, tax and numbering. A group's branches are consolidated in USD, server to server, without sharing a database.
- **Operated, not just hosted.** Automated daily database backups, a change history of every setting with before and after values, and a system health page for the background jobs.

## How a booking flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quote">
    Sales picks a buying rate for the route and container type and issues a quotation to the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Booking">
    The accepted quotation is converted into a booking. The route, equipment and the chosen route's charges carry over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and ship">
    Operations records the carrier booking, issues the Booking Confirmation, keeps the shipping timeline current and attaches documents as they arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bill of Lading">
    The House B/L and its container annexure are generated from the booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoice and cost">
    Invoices are created from the booking's sale (AR) lines in your base currency or USD; vendor costs sit on the same booking as cost (AP) lines, converted at the recorded exchange rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report">
    Gross profit, net income, aging and cash position update themselves from the entries above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Currencies

Invoices and books run in exactly two currencies: your **base currency** (SAR in Saudi Arabia, MYR in Malaysia, IDR in Indonesia) and **USD**. Costs recorded in a different currency are converted with the exchange rate captured on the line, so booking profitability compares like with like. The **Group** view puts every branch side by side, consolidated in USD.
