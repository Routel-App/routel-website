**Admin** is the link in the sidebar footer, open to the **Admin** role only; its badge is the System Health count. The menu is grouped into **Company**, **People**, **Finance**, **Integrations** and **System**.

## Company

**Company profile** has four cards: **Company Details** (name, address, phone, e-mail, website, registration number, plus a second-language name and address where invoices print in two languages), **Logo & print** (logo under 2 MB; **Print Theme** Classic or Banner), **Bank Details** (bank, accounts, SWIFT, branch address) and **Invoice Terms**.

**Numbering** sets the **Prefix** (taken from the company name until you set one) and an optional **Branch code** infix such as PKG, giving numbers like MIASPKG26000001. Each document type shows its next number and last issued; choose **Sequential** or **Jump ahead** to a starting number above the last issued. Numbers never go back, and each year starts its own series.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Some changes restart the server">
  A new prefix or branch code, or a new country and currency, applies with **Apply and restart**.
</Callout>

## People

**Users & roles**: **Add user** asks for a display name, e-mail and role; users can be renamed or removed later. The six roles are **Admin**, **Management**, **Accounting**, **Operations**, **Sales** and **Pricing**, and the **What each role can access** table shows what each opens. **Who can sign in** limits sign-in to **Allowed Email Domains** and **Allowed Specific Emails**. See [Roles & permissions](/docs/roles).

## Integrations

**Email accounts** connects the operations and finance mailboxes through OAuth (**Continue with** your provider), with **Reconnect** and **Disconnect**. See [Email sync](/docs/email-sync).

**AI Assistant** shows **Usage and limits**: messages and tokens over 30 days, the model, and **Messages per person per day** (0 means no limit). **What the assistant did** logs its actions, each with **Undo**. See [AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).

## System

**Change history** records who changed which setting, when, and the before and after values, including changes made outside the app. **Restore** puts a value back.

**Backups** are automated daily database backups stored in Cloudflare R2, retained indefinitely, running **every 24 hours**; a backup is stale after 30 hours.

**System health** lists the background jobs and when each counts as stale:

| Job | Stale after |
| --- | --- |
| db_backup | 30 hours |
| email_sync | 3 hours |
| finance_email_sync | 3 hours |
| zoho_token_cleanup | 192 hours |
| overdue_alerts | 8 hours |
| r2_reconciliation | 30 hours |

attachment_backfill is reported only when it fails. **R2 Storage** shows the file count and any orphaned or missing files; **Recent Runs** lists the latest runs.

**Group** appears only when this deployment is part of a group. See [Group](/docs/group).
