**Alerts** — "Items requiring immediate attention" — opens from the **Alerts** icon in the top bar. Any signed-in role can use it.

The side menu, **Needs attention**, lists the five alert types; each has its own URL (`/alerts/arriving-soon` and so on) and a count badge, so you can bookmark the one you check every morning. The lists load when you open the page and again only when you choose **Refresh** — there is no automatic refresh, and the top-bar icon itself carries no count.

## The five lists

An *active* booking here means any status from Booking Pending through Telex Status.

### Arriving Soon

Active bookings whose ETA falls between today and seven days from today. Columns: **Booking #**, **AR**, **Route**, **ETA**, **Status**.

### Payment Overdue

Issued invoices with money still owed and a due date already passed.

| Days overdue | Severity |
| --- | --- |
| More than 30 | Critical |
| More than 7 | High |
| Otherwise | Moderate |

Columns: **Invoice #**, **AR**, **Due date**, **Days overdue**, **Severity**, **Outstanding**.

### Unassigned

**Unassigned Shipments**: active bookings with no operations staff assigned. Open the booking and set the operations staff to clear it.

### Missing BL

**Missing BL Number**: bookings at SI Submitted, Vessel Loaded, Departed, In Transit, Arrived or Telex Status that still have no B/L number recorded. See [Bill of Lading](/docs/bill-of-lading).

### Follow-ups Due

Pending follow-ups dated today or earlier, oldest first, each marked with how late it is (for example *3d late*). Follow-ups are created on the booking or in the [Labeling Center](/docs/labeling-center).

<Callout kind="tip" title="Alerts versus the Dashboard">
  The Dashboard's "Needs your attention" block is a summary of counts; Alerts is the working list with the rows
  themselves. Keep Alerts open while you clear them, then Refresh to see what is left.
</Callout>
