Chasing payments usually means someone reading a shared inbox and manually matching a remittance email to an invoice. Routel's finance mailbox automates the matching, not the decision.

## The finance mailbox

A second mailbox — separate from the mailbox [Email Sync](/docs/email-sync) connects for day-to-day booking correspondence — is dedicated to finance and scanned automatically as mail arrives.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the mailbox">
    Admin connects the finance inbox the same way as the main one, via Zoho or Microsoft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Routel scans incoming mail">
    Every message is checked against open customer invoices and outstanding vendor bills for anything that looks
    like a payment reference, amount or remittance advice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A matching suggestion is proposed">
    When something looks like a match, it shows up as a suggested pairing between the email and the invoice or
    vendor bill it probably settles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A person confirms or rejects it">
    Confirming creates the actual payment record against that invoice or bill. Rejecting discards the suggestion.
    Nothing is settled automatically — every match needs a person to say yes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Receivables and payables in one place

**Receivables & Payables** lists what customers owe you (receivables) and what you owe vendors (payables) side by side, pulled from the same invoices and booking cost (AP) lines recorded elsewhere in the system — not a separate set of numbers to reconcile.

<Callout kind="note">
  This is about matching and recording payments, not moving money — Routel doesn't initiate bank transfers or
  payment runs.
</Callout>

## Through the assistant

"Show me unmatched payments from this week" or "Which invoices are still unpaid past terms?" both work as immediate answers — the assistant reads the same matching suggestions and open-item data as the hub.

<Callout kind="permission">
  Finance belongs to **Admin**, **Management** and **Accounting**: they work the receivables and payables and confirm
  or reject matching suggestions. **Admin** connects the finance mailbox.
</Callout>
