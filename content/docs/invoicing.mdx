An invoice is raised against a job. Line items usually come from the job's selling price (ocean freight, terminal handling, documentation and so on), but any line can be added or edited before the invoice is finalized.

## Draft, then final

A new invoice starts as a **draft** — line items can still be edited, and the PDF prints clearly watermarked **"DRAFT TAX INVOICE — NOT OFFICIAL"** so it's never mistaken for a billable document.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Finalizing requires the job's B/L number">
  An invoice can only be finalized once the job's Bill of Lading number is set. This is deliberate: it stops an
  invoice going out for a shipment that, on paper, hasn't actually moved yet.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job and choose Create invoice">
    The customer and currency default from the job. Lines are prefilled from the selling price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Adjust descriptions, quantities and prices. Add lines for extras agreed after quotation. It's still a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize">
    Once the job has its B/L number, finalizing assigns the real invoice number, posts to Accounts Receivable and
    revenue, and **locks the line items** — no further edits. The watermark comes off the PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Through the assistant">
  "Create an invoice for JOB-2026-0412 in MYR" produces a proposed draft invoice with the same prefilled lines.
  Nothing is finalized until you confirm. Uploading a vendor's own cost invoice works the same way in reverse: the
  assistant reads it and extracts line items to prefill a vendor cost or your own invoice. See
  [AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).
</Callout>

## Payments

Record a payment against one or more finalized invoices. Partial payments are supported; the invoice stays in aging until it's fully paid. Payments post to the bank or cash account you select and clear the receivable. Routel can also propose payment matches automatically from a dedicated mailbox — see [Finance (Receivables & Payables)](/docs/finance-receivables).

## Adjusting an issued invoice

A finalized invoice is never edited directly. If the amount needs to change after the fact, that goes through a formally numbered [Credit or Debit Note](/docs/credit-notes) applied against it instead.

## Trade credit

Letting a customer pay on terms, and how much credit they're extended, is handled by [Credit Applications](/docs/credit-applications) — a public application, staff approval, and an active agreement tied to the customer's [Business Partner](/docs/business-partners) record.

<Callout kind="permission">
  **Accounting** and **Admin** draft, finalize and record payments against invoices. **Management** can view
  everything on this page. **Sales** can see invoice status for their customers.
</Callout>

## Multi-currency behaviour

| Situation | What Routel does |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice in USD | Presented and paid in USD. Ledger records USD amount and MYR equivalent at the invoice date rate. |
| Vendor cost in USD on an MYR job | Converted at the exchange rate recorded on the job, so gross profit is computed in one currency. |
| Exchange differences | Recorded as a *Currency exchange* expense or gain when the settlement rate differs. See [Expenses & Payroll](/docs/expenses). |

## What posts where

| Action | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finalize invoice | Accounts Receivable | Revenue |
| Record payment | Bank / Cash | Accounts Receivable |
| Credit note | Revenue | Accounts Receivable |
| Debit note | Accounts Receivable | Revenue |
| Record vendor cost | Cost of Sales | Accounts Payable |

You never enter these journals yourself. They are shown here so you know what the General Ledger will contain.
