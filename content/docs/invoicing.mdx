An invoice is raised against a booking. Line items come from the booking's sale (AR) charge lines (ocean freight, terminal handling, documentation and so on), and any line can be added or edited while the invoice is still a draft.

## Draft, then final

A new invoice starts as a **draft** — line items can still be edited, and the PDF prints clearly watermarked **"DRAFT TAX INVOICE"** so it's never mistaken for a billable document.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Finalizing requires the booking's B/L number">
  An invoice can only be finalized once the booking has its Bill of Lading number (MBL). This is deliberate: it stops an
  invoice going out for a shipment that, on paper, hasn't actually moved yet.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking and choose Create Invoice">
    Draft invoices are created from the booking's sale (AR) charge lines, with the customer taken from the booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Adjust descriptions, quantities and prices. Add lines for extras agreed after quotation. It's still a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize">
    Once the booking has its B/L number, finalizing assigns the real invoice number, posts to Accounts Receivable and
    revenue, and **locks the line items** — no further edits. The watermark comes off the PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Through the assistant">
  "Create an invoice for booking NXS26000412 in MYR" produces a proposed draft invoice with the same prefilled
  lines. Nothing is finalized until you confirm. Uploading a vendor's own invoice works the same way in reverse: the
  AI reads it into proposed cost (AP) lines on the booking. See
  [AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).
</Callout>

## Payments

Record a payment against one or more finalized invoices. Partial payments are supported; the invoice stays in aging until it's fully paid. Payments post to the bank or cash account you select and clear the receivable. Routel can also propose payment matches automatically from a dedicated mailbox — see [Finance (Receivables & Payables)](/docs/finance-receivables).

## Adjusting an issued invoice

Only drafts are editable. A finalized invoice is never edited directly; if the amount needs to change after the fact, that goes through a formally numbered [Credit or Debit Note](/docs/credit-notes) applied against it instead.

## Trade credit

Letting a customer pay on terms, and how much credit they're extended, is handled by [Credit Applications](/docs/credit-applications) — a public application, staff approval, and an active agreement tied to the customer's [Business Partner](/docs/business-partners) record.

<Callout kind="permission">
  Finance belongs to **Admin**, **Management** and **Accounting**: they draft, finalize and record payments against
  invoices.
</Callout>

## Multi-currency behaviour

| Situation | What Routel does |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice in USD | Presented and paid in USD. Ledger records the USD amount and its base-currency equivalent at the invoice date rate. |
| Cost line in USD on a booking billed in MYR | Converted at the exchange rate recorded on the line, so profit is computed in one currency. |
| Exchange differences | Recorded as a *Currency exchange* expense or gain when the settlement rate differs. See [Expenses & Payroll](/docs/expenses). |

## What posts where

| Action | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finalize invoice | Accounts Receivable | Revenue |
| Record payment | Bank / Cash | Accounts Receivable |
| Credit note | Revenue | Accounts Receivable |
| Debit note | Accounts Receivable | Revenue |
| Record a vendor cost (AP) | Cost of Sales | Accounts Payable |

You never enter these journals yourself. They are shown here so you know what the General Ledger will contain.
