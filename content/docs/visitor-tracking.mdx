title: Website Visitor Tracking
description: See visitor activity on your own custom domain without wiring up separate analytics.
status: complete
---

When Routel runs on your own custom domain (for example `app.yourcompany.com`) instead of a shared Routel address, visitor activity on that site is tracked automatically. There is nothing separate to install or configure — it comes with the custom domain.

## What it shows

A simple view of who is visiting your branded site: how many visitors, which pages they land on, and when activity happens. It is meant to answer "is anyone looking at this" without standing up a separate analytics tool.

<Callout kind="note">
  This tracks visits to your public-facing branded site, not activity inside the Routel application itself — that
  already lives in each job's [audit log](/docs/shipments).
</Callout>

<Callout kind="permission">
  **Admin** and **Management** can view visitor activity.
</Callout>
