Your customers ask "where is my shipment" more than any other question. The public tracking page answers it without giving them a login, and without handing over anything commercially sensitive.

## Looking something up

No account is required, but a lookup needs **two** pieces of information together:

<Fields
  rows={[
    ["An exact reference", "The job number, B/L number or a container number — no partial matches and no search-by-keyword."],
    ["Proof of relationship", "Their company name or account number, matched against who the shipment actually belongs to."],
  ]}
/>

Requiring both prevents the page from being used to fish for other customers' shipments by guessing numbers.

## What it shows — and what it doesn't

The page shows the same status timeline used internally on the [Shipments & Jobs](/docs/shipments) module — booking confirmed, cargo received, vessel loaded, departed, arrived, and so on.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Deliberately limited">
  It never shows the master Bill of Lading, container numbers, vessel name, or exact operational dates — only the
  status stage. Those details stay inside the product for your own team and the customer's named contacts.
</Callout>

## Sharing the link

The tracking link is the same for every shipment — customers plug in their own reference and account number rather than you generating a unique link per job.

<Callout kind="permission">
  No login or role applies here — it's a public page, scoped entirely by the two-piece lookup above.
</Callout>
