A freight file is a pile of paper: booking confirmation, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, House and Master B/L, arrival notice, delivery order, vendor invoices. Routel keeps that pile with the booking it belongs to, so "where is the packing list for the Al Noor shipment?" has a one-click answer. File type is verified from the actual file content when it's uploaded — not just trusted from the filename or extension.

## Where documents live

Every booking has a **Documents** card. Two kinds of files appear there:

- **Uploaded** by your team: anything from the customer, the carrier or a vendor.
- **Generated** by Routel: the Booking Confirmation and its revisions, the House B/L and annexure, customer invoices, credit and debit notes. These are attached automatically when they are produced.

Partner records have a documents area too, for company-level files such as agreements and registration documents.

## Uploading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking and go to Documents">
    Drag files in, or choose them. PDFs, images and office documents are accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label the document">
    Pick a type (booking confirmation, packing list, commercial invoice, and so on) so it is easy to find later. Add a
    note if useful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The upload is recorded with your name and the time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finding and downloading

From the booking, filter by type or search by name. Across bookings, the assistant is the fastest route: "Find the packing list for the Al Noor shipment" or "Send me the House B/L for booking NXS26000412" returns the file directly, subject to your role.

## Versions and corrections

Uploading a corrected document does not overwrite the old one. Both are kept, the newest is marked current, and you can see who replaced what. For generated documents, regenerating produces a new version the same way.

## Retention

Documents stay with the booking after the shipment is closed and remain downloadable for as long as the booking exists. Nothing is purged automatically.

## Incoming attachments, reviewed automatically

A second layer sits in front of the manual upload flow. When mail arrives through [Email Sync](/docs/email-sync), the AI reads each attachment into the **Documents** review queue and classifies it: B/L, booking confirmation, booking request, vendor invoice, or other. It then suggests the booking it belongs to, matching on the booking number first, then the B/L number, then a container number.

From the queue, the reviewer picks the action that fits:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve & Create Booking** | Creates a new booking from the document, for example a customer's booking request. |
| **Approve & Update Booking** | Updates the matched booking with what the document says, for example a carrier's confirmation or B/L. |
| **Approve & Create Invoice** | Records the invoice in the document (typically a vendor invoice) against the matched booking. |
| **Link to Booking** | Simply files the document on a booking you choose. |

<Callout kind="warning" title="Suggested, never automatic">
  Nothing from this queue is filed on its own. A person approves every one, and can reject a suggestion or pick a
  different booking, before the document is attached anywhere.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="permission">
  Any signed-in staff member can view and download a booking's documents, and upload or label new ones. Clearing the
  AI review queue is part of normal day-to-day use, not a separate restricted role.
</Callout>
