---
title: Document Management
description: Upload, attach and download documents per shipment.
status: stub
---

Every job has a documents area. Upload booking confirmations, packing lists, commercial invoices, certificates or anything else, and they stay with the shipment. Documents generated by Routel, such as the House B/L, quotation and invoice PDFs, are attached automatically.

Documents can be searched and downloaded from the job page or by asking the [assistant](/docs/ai-assistant), for example "find the packing list for the Al Noor shipment".

<Callout kind="permission">
  Anyone who can open a job can view and download its documents. **Operations** and **Admin** upload and remove them.
</Callout>
