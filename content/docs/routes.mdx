**Routes** — "Pricing and performance for every shipping route" — has two views: **Route Rates** at `/routes` and **Route Analytics** at `/routes/analytics`. It is locked on plans that exclude it.

## Route Rates

Every signed-in role can add and edit rates. The list shows 50 per page with sortable columns **Route**, **AP**, **Shipping line**, **Container**, **Validity** (start – end), **Status** and **Buying price**. Search covers POL, POD, AP and shipping line; filter by status (**All**, **Active**, **Expiring soon**, **Expired**) or AP.

Status counts the days from today to the end date; a rate is valid through its end date:

| Days left | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer than 0 | Expired |
| 0 to 14 | Expiring |
| More than 14 | Active |

### Extend validity and Deactivate

**Extend validity** asks for a **New end date**, saves it and re-activates the rate. **Deactivate** is soft: the rate is marked inactive and its row stays, faded; Extend validity brings it back.

### Import from Excel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Import from Excel">
    Accepted files: .xlsx, .xls or .csv. Headers are matched by synonym: POL/Origin/From; POD/Destination/To; Shipping line/Carrier/Line/Vendor; Container/Size/Type; Price/Rate/Cost/Amount/Sea freight; Currency/Ccy; Valid From/Start date/Effective date; Valid To/End date/Expiry/Valid until; Notes/Remarks/Comments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the defaults">
    Each row needs POL, POD, a price and a valid-to date, or it is skipped and reported. Valid-from defaults to today, currency to USD (an unknown currency becomes USD with a warning) and container to 20GP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    A row matching an existing rate on POL, POD, container and shipping line updates it; otherwise a rate is created; several matches mean the row is skipped. The result lists New rates created, Existing rates updated and the rows needing a look.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note" title="Imports never delete">
  An import only creates or updates. Rates not in the file are left as they are.
</Callout>

## Route Analytics

Analytics is part of the Reports module, open to **Admin**, **Management** and **Accounting**. Pick **Last 3, 6, 12 or 24 months** (default 12): bookings created since the same day that many months ago.

- Tiles: **Total Shipments**, **Unique Routes**, **Origins (POL)**, **Destinations (POD)**.
- **Monthly Shipment Volume**: a bar chart by ETD month (creation month when there is no ETD).
- **Top Routes by Volume**: the top 20 lanes with Shipments, Revenue (base currency) and **Margin**, coloured green above 25%, blue above 10%, amber above 0%, otherwise red. Click a route for a card with Shipments, Margin, Revenue / Cost / Profit and a **Monthly trend**.
- **Top Origins (POL)** and **Top Destinations (POD)**: the top 10 of each.
