Routel generates a **House Bill of Lading** (HBL) from the booking. The document is laid out like a carrier's B/L, not like a company letterhead: the boxes, numbering and terms are where a consignee, bank or customs officer expects them to be.

## What is generated

- **The B/L itself.** Shipper, consignee, notify party, vessel and voyage, port of loading, port of discharge, place of receipt and delivery, marks and numbers, description of goods, gross weight and measurement, incoterms, freight terms, number of originals, place and date of issue.
- **The annexure / container sheet.** One row per container with container number, type, seal number, package count and description, printed as a continuation sheet referencing the B/L number.

Both are produced as a single PDF and attached to the booking's documents.

## Before you generate

The B/L is only as good as the booking's shipment details. Check:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Parties">
    Shipper, consignee and notify party names and addresses as they should appear on the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route and vessel">
    POL, POD, vessel and voyage on the booking match the carrier's confirmation (use **Read Carrier Confirmation** to compare).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Containers">
    Containers and container type are complete. They print on the annexure exactly as entered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cargo description">
    Marks, description of goods, gross weight and measurement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generating

Open the booking and choose **Generate Bill of Lading** in the header. Routel assigns the HBL number if the booking does not have one yet, renders the PDF, and attaches it to the booking. Regenerating after a correction replaces the draft; issued originals are kept for the record.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Regenerate after edits">
  The PDF is a snapshot. If you change a container or party after generating, generate again so the document matches
  the booking.
</Callout>

## Numbering

The House B/L number is assigned by Routel from your prefix and sequence. The carrier's Master B/L number (MBL) is entered on the booking's shipment details when the carrier issues it — or read from the carrier's B/L with **Upload BL** — and is required before an [invoice](/docs/invoicing) can be finalized.

## Through the assistant

"Generate the House B/L for booking NXS26000412" produces the same PDF, subject to the same role check, and attaches it to the booking. "Send me the B/L for the Al Noor shipment" finds and returns the attached document. See [AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).

<Callout kind="permission">
  **Admin**, **Management** and **Operations** generate the B/L. Everyone who can open the booking can download it.
</Callout>
