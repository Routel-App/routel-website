Routel keeps a record for every company you work with. **Customers** are who you invoice. **Vendors** are carriers, co-loaders, haulers, customs brokers and anyone else whose costs land on your jobs. The records are deliberately light: enough to run operations and finance, without turning into a CRM project.

## Customers

The customer page lists everything about the relationship: quotations, jobs, invoices, payments, notes and the running balance. From here you can raise a quotation, open any job, or generate a statement.

### Credit limits and applications

Give a customer a limit and Routel shows the current exposure, open invoices plus jobs in progress, whenever someone creates a job or an invoice for them. A **credit application** records a request to set or raise the limit; Accounting reviews and approves it, and the new limit takes effect on the record. See [Invoicing & Finance](/docs/invoicing).

## Vendor costs per job

A vendor cost is a line on a job for something a vendor charged you for that shipment.

<Fields
  rows={[
    ["Vendor", "Who charged it."],
    ["Description", "Ocean freight, THC, haulage, customs clearance and so on."],
    ["Currency and amount", "In the vendor's currency."],
    ["Exchange rate", "The rate used to convert to MYR. Defaults from the job; can be overridden per line."],
    ["MYR equivalent", "Computed. This is what feeds gross profit."],
    ["Vendor invoice reference", "The vendor's invoice number, and the PDF attached."],
    ["Status", "Unpaid or paid, with the payment date."],
  ]}
/>

Recording costs promptly is what makes the job P&L trustworthy. The job page shows revenue, converted cost, gross profit and margin the moment a cost is saved.

</Callout>

## Statements of account

A **statement of account** (SOA) summarises the relationship for a period: opening balance, invoices, payments, notes and closing balance for a customer; costs and payments for a vendor. Statements are generated as PDF from the partner record, from the Receivables & Payables hub, or by asking the assistant: "Download Al Noor's statement for August."

## Through the assistant

"Create a customer called Al Noor Trading LLC, 30-day terms, USD" or "Which vendors do we owe more than USD 5,000?" both work, the first as a proposal to confirm, the second as an immediate answer.

</Callout>
