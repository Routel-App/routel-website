## Customers

A customer record holds contact details, payment terms and credit limit, and lists the customer's quotations, jobs and invoices. A **statement of account** for any period can be generated as a PDF from the record, from the Receivables & Payables hub, or by asking the assistant.

## Vendors

Vendor records hold contact details and the costs recorded against them across jobs. A vendor statement shows what has been recorded and what has been paid.

## Vendor costs per job

A vendor cost is recorded on a job in the vendor's currency with an exchange rate. Routel converts it so gross profit on the job is computed in one currency. Upload the vendor's invoice to the [assistant](/docs/ai-assistant) to have the line items extracted into a proposed cost.

<Callout kind="permission">
  **Accounting** and **Admin** manage partner records and statements. **Operations** records vendor costs on jobs.
</Callout>
