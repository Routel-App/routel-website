Not every follow-up deserves a full task-management system — sometimes you just need to flag a record and come back to it. The Labeling Center is that flag, built into the records themselves.

## What you can tag

<Fields
  rows={[
    ["Job", "A shipment that needs a second look."],
    ["Invoice", "Something to chase or double-check before it's sent."],

  ]}
/>

## A tag carries three things

<Steps>
  <Step title="A note">
    Free text: what needs attention and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A follow-up date">
    When to come back to it. Overdue tags are easy to spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An assignee">
    Who owns following up — doesn't have to be the person who created the tag.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tags show up directly on the record they're attached to, so whoever opens that job or invoice sees the flag in context — no separate list to remember to check, though an assignee can also see everything tagged to them in one place.

## Through the assistant

"Tag JOB-2026-0412 for follow-up Friday, ask the customer for the signed SI" proposes a tag with the note, date and assignee filled in for you to confirm.

<Callout kind="permission">
  Any signed-in staff member can tag a record they can already open, and assign it to anyone with access to that
  record.
</Callout>
