## Your first ten minutes

When an admin signs in before setup is complete, the assistant opens by itself (once per browser) with **Welcome to Routel — let's set up your company** and a progress bar such as **3 of 11**. Choose **Start setup** and it walks you through the checklist below. Nothing is saved until you confirm each step. A warning dot on the assistant button counts the details still missing.

| # | Step | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Company name | Yes |
| 2 | Company name in a second language | Where invoices print in two languages |
| 3 | Company registration number | Yes |
| 4 | Document numbering | No — done once reviewed or a document is issued |
| 5 | Company logo | No |
| 6 | Company address | Yes |
| 7 | Company phone or e-mail | Yes |
| 8 | Tax registration number | No |
| 9 | Bank details for invoices | No |
| 10 | Team members and their roles | No — done with more than one user, or a plan without roles |
| 11 | Charge types | No |

Setup is complete when every required step is done.

<Callout kind="note" title="Assistant modes">
  The assistant runs in **full** mode when your plan includes it (Freight Forwarder Growth or Enterprise). On other plans
  it runs in **onboarding** mode — setup only, admins only — while setup is incomplete and for 30 days after the first
  account was created. After that it is **off** until the plan includes it.
</Callout>

## Country and currency

Currency is not a checklist step. While the country is still the default, the dashboard shows a card: **1 setting to choose: this branch's country and currency, before the first booking**. It opens **Settings → Currency & tax**, where you pick the country and base currency; applying restarts the server. The choice is fixed once the first booking or invoice exists.

## First actions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Users & roles">
    In Admin → Users & roles, add each team member with a display name, e-mail and role. See [Roles & permissions](/docs/roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business partners">
    Add your customers and vendors under Business Partners. See [Business Partners](/docs/business-partners).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route rates">
    Enter buying prices under Routes, or choose Import from Excel to load a rate sheet in one go. See [Routes](/docs/routes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charge items">
    Review the catalog of charge names used on quotations, bookings and invoices under Admin → Charge items.
  </Step>
</Steps>
