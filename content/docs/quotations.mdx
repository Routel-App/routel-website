## Route rates

A **route rate** is a vendor's buying price for a lane: origin port, destination port, container type, currency and amount, with a **validity window**. Rates that have expired are hidden from quotation by default so nobody quotes on a dead price.

## Quotations

A quotation is issued to a customer for a specific route and equipment, priced on top of a selected route rate. It is rendered as a PDF and tracked until it is accepted, declined or expires.

## Converting to a job

An accepted quotation converts into a job with one action. Customer, route, equipment and pricing carry across, so Operations starts from a complete record. See [Shipments & Jobs](/docs/shipments).

<Callout kind="permission">
  **Sales** and **Admin** manage route rates and quotations. **Operations** converts accepted quotations into jobs.
</Callout>
