The **Dashboard** is the page you land on after signing in: "A snapshot of bookings, revenue, and what needs a look today." Any signed-in role can open it, and it is included on the free plan. Its sections appear in the order below.

## Needs your attention

This block shows only when something is listed. Each card is a link, and a card whose source your role cannot read is left out.

| Card | Opens | Shows when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *N* Overdue Invoices (red) | Invoices, filtered to overdue | An issued invoice is past its due date with money owed |
| *N* Pending Invoices | Invoices | Invoices are draft, unpaid or final |
| *N* Labels Due Today / Overdue | Labeling Center | A label is due today or earlier |
| *N* Documents Pending Review | Documents | Incoming files wait in the review queue |
| *N* Payment Suggestions to Review | Receivables & Payables inbox | The finance mailbox produced suggestions |
| *N* Pending Business Partner Registrations | Business Partners | A partner self-registered and awaits approval |
| 1 database backup is missing or overdue (red) | Admin → Backups | No backup has completed in the last 30 hours |
| 1 setting to choose: this branch's country and currency, before the first booking | Admin → Currency & tax | The country is still the default |
| *N* background jobs are failing or overdue — check System Health (red) | Admin → System health | A job has failed or is stale ("Could not check system health" if the check itself fails) |

<Callout kind="note" title="How the figures are counted">
  All four tiles cover all time up to today. Revenue comes from issued invoices by invoice date, excludes sales tax,
  and is net of credit and debit notes. Outstanding and overdue amounts are as of today.
</Callout>

## Recent Bookings

The 8 newest bookings by creation date, each with its number, status, customer, POL → POD and ETD/ETA. **View all** opens the full list. See [Bookings](/docs/bookings).

## Booking Pipeline

A count of bookings per status, all time, each with a share bar.

## AR Balances

Shown when there is data: one row per business partner, sorted by name, with **Outstanding** per currency or **All settled**. The **Total** line is invoiced amounts plus debit notes. **View all** opens Business Partners.

<Callout kind="permission">
  Every signed-in role can open the Dashboard. The attention cards follow your other permissions: if you cannot read
  Invoices, the invoice cards are not shown. See [Roles & permissions](/docs/roles).
</Callout>
