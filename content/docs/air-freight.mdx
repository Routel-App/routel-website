Air freight is a business activity an admin opens in **Settings → Business activities**. Once it's open, **New Booking** offers **Sea** or **Air**. The mode is fixed once the booking is created.

## Express or airline cargo

The AP partner on the booking decides which kind of air booking it is:

| Kind | When | Document |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Express** | The AP is marked as an express carrier (DHL, FedEx, Aramex, UPS, SMSA…) | The carrier's own waybill. Routel keeps its tracking number with a link to the carrier's tracking page. |
| **Airline cargo** | Any other AP | A Master Air Waybill (MAWB) from the airline and your own House Air Waybill (HAWB). |

## The MAWB

Type the 11-digit MAWB. Routel checks the IATA check digit, names the airline from its three-digit prefix (for example 065 Saudia, 157 Qatar Airways, 176 Emirates) and refuses a wrong number. It is stored as `157-12345675`.

The airport and airline lists come pre-filled (64 airports, including RUH, JED and DMM, and 43 airlines). An admin can add more.

## Pieces and chargeable weight

Enter each line of pieces with its dimensions in centimetres and its gross weight.

Freight and surcharges are charged per kg, and the AWB fee per AWB. When the pieces change, every per-kg line moves to the new chargeable weight. Lines already on an issued invoice or a paid AP keep their quantity, and Routel tells you which ones it left.

## Statuses

Air bookings use the air list: Booking Pending, Booking Confirmed, Cargo Received, Departed, In Transit, Arrived, Customs Clearance, Delivered, or Cancelled.

## The House Air Waybill

For airline cargo, one HAWB per booking.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    From the booking. Shipper, consignee, notify party, airports, flight and date, handling information and goods come from the booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the draft">
    Agent's IATA code, routing, prepaid or collect, declared values, insurance, rate class, rate per kg, other charges and place of issue. The weight charge is chargeable weight × rate per kg.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue">
    Needs the shipper, the consignee, both airports and the pieces. An issued HAWB can't be edited; a draft prints with a DRAFT mark.
  </Step>
</Steps>

It prints on blank A4 in the IATA air waybill layout, with your logo as issuing agent. Express bookings don't get a HAWB, because the carrier's waybill is their document.

## Air allocations

**Air allocations** tracks block space bought from airlines.

- **An allocation:** the airline, origin and destination, an optional flight, the kilos per week, hard or soft (soft space has release days), the rate per kg and currency, and the validity period.
- **Bookings count automatically:** an airline-cargo booking with the same airline, route and flight, in the period, counts against its week (Monday to Sunday, by flight date, else ETD), by chargeable weight.
- **Each week shows** capacity, used, remaining and utilisation. Hard space also shows the unsold cost; soft space shows its release-by date.
- **Alerts** for this week and next: hard space under 75% sold, soft space to release soon, filling and full. They appear in Alerts and in the assistant's daily brief.
- **Customers to call:** AR partners who shipped airline cargo on the route in the last 180 days and haven't booked that week.
- **AI reads the agreement:** upload the airline's allocation agreement as a PDF, scan or photo, and the AI fills in the new allocation. Nothing is saved until you save it.

A quotation on a route with an allocation suggests that space first.
