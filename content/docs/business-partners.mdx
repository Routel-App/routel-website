Routel keeps **one record** for every company you work with — there's no separate "add a customer" step and "add a vendor" step. Which side of a deal a partner plays is decided per transaction: the same company can be invoiced on one booking and billed as a vendor on another, without two separate records drifting out of sync.

The partner page lists everything about the relationship from both directions: quotations and invoices where they're the customer, route rates and costs where they're the vendor, bookings either way, and a combined running balance.

## Combined AR + AP ledger

Because a partner can sit on both sides of your books, Routel keeps one ledger per partner that nets what they owe you (AR, from invoices) against what you owe them (AP, from vendor costs) — instead of two disconnected balances you'd have to add up by hand.

## Statements of account

A **statement of account** (SOA) summarises the relationship for a period — opening balance, invoices, payments, costs, notes and closing balance — generated as a PDF from the partner record, from the [Finance hub](/docs/finance-receivables), or by asking the assistant: "Download Al Noor's statement for August."

## Vendor costs per booking

A vendor cost is a cost (AP) line in a booking's charges for something a partner charged you for that shipment, acting as a vendor.

<Fields

    ["Currency and amount", "In the vendor's currency."],
    ["Exchange rate", "The rate used to convert to your base currency (e.g. MYR). Set per line."],
    ["Base-currency equivalent", "Computed. This is what feeds the booking's Profit Summary and gross profit."],

    ["Status", "Unpaid or paid, with the payment date."],
  ]}
/>

Recording costs promptly is what makes booking profitability trustworthy. The booking's **Profit Summary** shows AP, AR and net the moment a cost is saved.

  line items, currency and total into proposed cost lines. Check them against the paper and confirm. See
  [AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).
</Callout>

## Credit limits

Give a partner a limit and Routel shows current exposure — open invoices plus bookings in progress — whenever someone creates a booking or an invoice for them. Setting or raising a limit goes through [Credit Applications](/docs/credit-applications), not a direct edit.

## Through the assistant

"Create a business partner called Al Noor Trading LLC, 30-day terms, USD" or "Which partners do we owe more than USD 5,000?" both work, the first as a proposal to confirm, the second as an immediate answer.

</Callout>
