---
title: Bookings
description: Record and manage vessel bookings against a job.
status: stub
---

A **booking** records the carrier's confirmation for a job: vessel, voyage, booking reference, cut-offs and the equipment confirmed. It is created from the job page and its vessel and voyage details flow to the job and to the [Bill of Lading](/docs/bill-of-lading).

Attach the carrier's booking confirmation to the job so it travels with the shipment. See [Document Management](/docs/documents).

<Callout kind="permission">
  **Operations** and **Admin** create and edit bookings.
</Callout>
