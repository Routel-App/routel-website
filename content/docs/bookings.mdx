A **booking** is one shipment for one customer, and it is the one core record in Routel. Everything else hangs off it: the carrier's space confirmation, the containers, the Bill of Lading, the charges, the invoices and the documents. Its number is the booking number, and it is the number printed on the B/L, the invoice and statements. When someone asks "where is the Al Noor shipment?", the booking is the page they open.

## Creating a booking

There are three ways a booking comes into existence:

1. **From a quotation.** The normal path. Open an accepted quotation and choose **Convert to Booking**. If the quotation offered several routes, you pick one and only that route's charges are copied across. The quotation then becomes read-only, with a "Converted" badge and a link to the booking. See [Quotations & Routes](/docs/quotations).
2. **Directly.** Choose **New Booking** from the Bookings list when there was no formal quotation.
3. **Through the assistant.** Describe the shipment in a sentence; the assistant proposes the booking and waits for confirmation. See [AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Bookings and choose New Booking">
    Pick the customer. If the customer has a credit limit, the current exposure is shown so you can see it before you commit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the route and cargo">
    Port of loading, port of discharge, containers and container type, incoterms and a cargo description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The booking number is assigned and the status starts at <em>Booking Pending</em>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shipping timeline

The **Shipping timeline** card keeps the carrier's dates in one place: **Gate-in cut-off, SI cut-off, ETD, SOB, ATD, ETA and ATA**. Update them as the carrier updates you; every change is recorded in the activity log.

## Carrier booking

The **Carrier Booking** card records the carrier's side of the deal: the **shipping line**, the **Carrier Booking No.**, the containers confirmed, the **notify party** and any booking notes.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Read Carrier Confirmation">
  Choose **Read Carrier Confirmation** and point it at the carrier's confirmation PDF or a linked e-mail. The AI reads
  it and shows what it found next to what the booking already holds; you tick the values to save. Nothing is written
  until you do.
</Callout>

## Booking Confirmation and revisions

**Booking Confirmation** in the page header issues your confirmation to the customer. The first one issued is the original. If anything changes on the booking afterwards, the next one issued is a **Revised Booking Confirmation — Revision N**, numbered in sequence, so the customer always knows which version is current. Every issue is recorded in the activity log.

## Statuses

Each booking moves through an 11-status lifecycle, from booking pending to shipment closed. The status timeline on the booking page shows the current status, the ones already passed and when each was reached.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Booking Pending | Created, waiting for the carrier to confirm space. |
| Booking Confirmed | The carrier has confirmed the booking. |
| Gate In | The container has been gated in at the terminal. |
| SI Submitted | Shipping instructions sent to the carrier. |
| Vessel Loaded | The container is on board. |
| Departed | The vessel has sailed. |
| In Transit | On the way to the port of discharge. |
| Arrived | The vessel has arrived at the port of discharge. |
| Telex Status | Release of the cargo is being handled (telex release). |
| Shipment Closed | The shipment is complete. |
| Cancelled | The booking was cancelled. Possible at any point. |

The same status badge appears wherever the booking shows up: the Bookings list, the Dashboard, partner views, and the [public tracking page](/docs/tracking) your own customers can check without logging in.

## Charges and profit summary

**Booking Charges** holds two kinds of line: **cost (AP)** lines for what carriers, haulers, customs brokers and other vendors charge you, and **sale (AR)** lines for what you bill the customer. Each line keeps its own currency and exchange rate.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Let the AI read the vendor invoice">
  Upload a vendor's invoice and the AI reads it into proposed cost lines on the booking, for you to review and confirm.
</Callout>

The **Profit Summary** card shows **AP · AR · Net** for the booking. It is visible to finance roles only.

## Create invoice

**Create Invoice** in the header creates draft invoices from the booking's sale (AR) charge lines. The invoices then appear on the booking's **Invoices** card. Drafting, finalizing and payment are covered in [Invoicing](/docs/invoicing).

## Generate the Bill of Lading

**Generate Bill of Lading** in the header produces the House B/L and container annexure from the booking's own data. Keep the shipment details accurate: parties, vessel, containers and cargo description all print exactly as entered. See [Bill of Lading](/docs/bill-of-lading).

## Activity log

Every field change and every confirmation issue is recorded in the **Activity log**: the person, the time and the before/after values, including anything confirmed through the assistant. It cannot be edited.

## Labels, tasks and follow-ups

The booking page carries its own **Labels**, **Tasks** and **Follow-ups**, so an open question with the carrier or a reminder to chase the signed SI sits on the record it is about. See [Labeling Center](/docs/labeling-center).

## Documents

The **Documents** card holds every file on the booking: what your team uploads, what Routel generates (the B/L, invoices, confirmations), and incoming e-mail attachments approved from the review queue. See [Document Management](/docs/documents).

<Callout kind="permission">
  Every signed-in member of staff can read bookings. **Admin**, **Management**, **Operations** and **Sales** can create
  and edit them. The Profit Summary is limited to finance roles, and the Bill of Lading to Admin, Management and
  Operations. See [Roles & permissions](/docs/roles).
</Callout>
