The assistant is the fastest way to work in Routel. Instead of finding the right screen and filling in a form, you say what you need in a sentence: "quote Al Noor for a 40HC to Jebel Ali", "who owes us the most?", "record the carrier's confirmation on this booking". The assistant works it out, calls the same actions the screens use, and puts the result in front of you.

It does two kinds of work:

- **Reading** runs immediately: searching, looking up a booking or an invoice, reports, statements, downloads, the alerts, your daily brief.
- **Writing** is always a proposal: a card that shows exactly what will be created or changed. Nothing is saved until you press **Confirm**.

## Opening it

- From the top bar of any page: **Ask the Routel assistant…** (an icon on phones).
- From the keyboard: **Ctrl + G** on Windows, **⌘ + G** on Mac, from anywhere in Routel.
- From the Help Center, which can hand your question straight to the assistant.
- When an admin signs in to a company that isn't set up yet, it opens by itself, once per browser, to run the [guided setup](/docs/getting-started).

**It knows where you are.** On a booking, a business partner, a quotation, an invoice or a credit note, "this" means that record: "add a follow-up on this for Thursday", "issue the invoice for this booking". It also follows the conversation, so after talking about a partner or a booking you can say "their balance" or "its status" without repeating the name.

## Two rules that make it safe on real data

1. **It has your permissions, not its own.** It acts as you and is subject to the same [role-based access](/docs/roles) as every screen. If your role can't do something on the screen, it can't do it in the chat either, and it tells you why.
2. **It never saves without you.** Every action that writes data stops as a proposal card. You confirm, edit or cancel. There is no setting that lets it skip this.

Replies are short and in plain words. When there are a few known choices (a role, a container type, which of several matches) it shows them as buttons you can tap. When a guide answer points to a screen, the page name is a link, and some answers carry a **Show me on the screen** button.

## Reading your documents

Attach a file to the message with the paperclip, and the assistant reads it before deciding what to do.

- **What it reads:** PDF, images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF), Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV and Word (.docx), up to 20 MB. Scanned PDFs and photos are read too.
- **A carrier's booking confirmation** becomes an update to the booking: vessel and voyage, cut-offs, the carrier's booking number.
- **A vendor's invoice** becomes cost lines on the booking, each with its currency.
- **A rate sheet** in Excel or CSV becomes a single card that says how many rates will be added and how many updated, without duplicates.
- **A receipt or a bill** for company overhead becomes an expense.
- **A registration certificate or a letterhead**, during setup, fills in several company details at once.
- **Any other document** can simply be filed on a booking.

## The confirmation card

<Steps>
  <Step title="You ask">
    In a sentence, in any language. If something is missing it asks, rather than guessing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It proposes">
    A card shows every field, line and amount that will be saved, with names instead of internal codes. Lists of
    lines show as a table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You decide">
    **Confirm** saves it. **Edit** opens the fields so you can correct them first. **Cancel** discards it. Nothing is
    written until one of these is pressed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A few details that matter on real data:

- **Checked again on save.** When you confirm, Routel validates the values and your role again, exactly as the screen would.
- **A double click does nothing twice.** The Confirm button locks while it runs, and a second confirm of the same card returns the first result.
- **Money is allocated exactly.** Before recording a receipt or a payment it looks up what is owed and proposes the amount for each document, never a lump sum.
- **Data stays in English.** Routel stores its operational data in English, so it writes port names, cargo descriptions and other values in standard English even when you chat in another language. If a value on a card isn't in English, the card highlights it before you confirm.
- **It knows what you decided.** Confirmed, cancelled or failed, the outcome goes back into the conversation, so it never proposes the same thing twice without being asked.

<Callout kind="note" title="No silent execution">
  The assistant never tells you something was created before you confirm it. A booking, an invoice or a payment is
  never saved because a sentence was misread.
</Callout>

## Undo

For ten minutes after confirming, a note, a task, a follow-up, an e-mail filed to a booking or a draft quotation can be undone, by the person who confirmed it or by an admin. Anything that moved money or received a document number is reversed the proper way, with its own document, such as a credit note.

## Your daily brief

When a conversation is empty, the assistant opens with a **Today** card, built straight from your data: your tasks and follow-ups due, arrivals in the next seven days, bookings still missing a Master B/L or an operations owner, overdue invoices, drafts older than a week, vendor bills due, delivered bookings not yet invoiced and quotations about to expire. Each person sees what their role covers. Ask "what needs me today?" to get it at any time.

## Multilingual

The assistant is multilingual. Write in the language you work in and it replies in the same language. You can switch languages mid-conversation or mix them in one sentence, and it still understands. Whatever the language of the chat, the data it saves stays in English, so every screen, document and report reads the same for everyone.

## Memory and history

- **Preferences it keeps.** Tell it "remember that our usual trucker is Swift Haulage" or "always show amounts in USD", and it keeps the note across conversations. Ask it to list what it remembers, or to forget one note or all of them. Notes are private to you: up to 50, of 300 characters each.
- **What it refuses to remember.** Anything that looks like a password, a key, a PIN or a card number. It also won't save a note after reading outside material in the same conversation, such as an uploaded file or an e-mail.
- **Conversation history.** The panel reopens your last conversation. **Conversation history** lists your last 30, and **New chat** starts a fresh one.

## Who can do what

The assistant follows the same role map as the screens:

| Area | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| Bookings | Admin, Management, Operations, Sales |
| Quotations | Admin, Management, Sales, Pricing |
| Route rates | Admin, Management, Operations, Sales, Pricing |
| House B/L | Admin, Management, Operations |
| Vendor costs on bookings | Admin, Management, Accounting, Operations |
| Invoices, receipts, payments, credit notes, statements | Admin, Management, Accounting |
| Reports | Admin, Management, Accounting |
| Expenses | Admin, Management |
| Notes, tasks, follow-ups, alerts | Everyone |

A few actions are narrower: granting credit terms or a credit limit and reviewing credit applications are for Admin and Management; company setup, users and roles, registrations and leads are for Admin. Balances, invoice amounts and booking profit are hidden from roles outside finance.

| You are | You ask | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operations | "Create an invoice for this booking" | Refused with the reason: invoicing belongs to the finance roles. |
| Sales | "Show me all overdue invoices" | Refused with the reason: finance is for Admin, Management and Accounting. |
| Accounting | "Record the payment from Al Noor" | A card with the exact allocation per invoice, then confirm. |
| Management | "Customer profitability for Q3" | The report runs immediately. |

## For admins

**Settings → AI Assistant** shows usage for the last 30 days, sets a **daily message limit per person** (200 by default, 0 for no limit), and lists **What the assistant did**: every confirmed action, who confirmed it, the result, and Undo where it applies.

Whether the assistant is fully on depends on your plan. On plans without it, it still helps the admin set the company up during the first 30 days. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).

## What it does not do

- It does not send e-mails or messages to customers or vendors.
- It does not delete bookings, invoices, partners or users. The only removal is the ten-minute undo above.
- It does not act on its own. It only does something when someone asks.
- It does not approve documents from the review queue; that is done on the Documents page.
- It does not change the country, currency or time zone.

## Privacy and audit

Each person's conversations are their own. Requests to the AI model are sent with storage turned off at the provider; your conversation history is kept in Routel. Every action confirmed through the assistant is logged against the person who confirmed it, with the same detail as a change made on the screen. See the activity log on the record.
