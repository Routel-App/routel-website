---
title: AI Assistant
description: A chat assistant inside Routel that performs real actions, in Arabic or English, with a human confirmation before anything is saved.
status: complete
---

The assistant lives inside Routel as a chat panel. You describe what you want in Arabic or English, and it does the work through the same actions the interface uses: creating a job, drafting an invoice, recording an expense, running a report or fetching a document.

Two rules make it safe to use on real data:

1. **It has your permissions, not its own.** The assistant acts as the signed-in user and is subject to the same [role-based access](/docs/roles) as every screen.
2. **It never saves without you.** Any action that writes data is shown as a proposal. You review it and confirm, edit or cancel. Reads (reports, searches, downloads) run immediately.

## What it can do

| Area | Examples of what to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Jobs and bookings | "Create a job for Al Noor Trading, Port Klang to Jebel Ali, 2 × 40HC, ETD 12 October." "Update the ETA on JOB-2026-0412 to 28 October." "Record the booking on MSC Isabella voyage 2638W." |
| Quotations and rates | "Quote Al Noor for a 40HC Port Klang to Jeddah using the Maersk rate." "Add a route rate: Port Klang to Jebel Ali, 40HC, USD 1,450, valid to end of December." |
| Invoices | "Create an invoice for JOB-2026-0412 in MYR." |
| Expenses | "Record September office rent, MYR 6,500." |
| Vendor costs | Upload a vendor invoice PDF and ask: "Propose the vendor cost for this on JOB-2026-0412." The assistant extracts the line items. |
| Statements | "Download Al Noor's statement of account as PDF." "Vendor statement for Maersk for August." |
| Reports | "Which invoices are overdue?" "Show AR aging." "Customer profitability for Q3." "What's our cash position?" |
| Documents | "Find the packing list for the Al Noor shipment." "Send me the House B/L for JOB-2026-0412." |

## The confirmation flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="You ask">
    In Arabic, English or a mix. The assistant asks for anything it is missing rather than guessing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It proposes">
    A card shows exactly what will be created or changed: every field, every line, every amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You decide">
    **Confirm** saves it and records your name in the audit log. **Edit** lets you adjust before saving. **Cancel**
    discards it. Nothing happens until one of these is pressed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note" title="No silent execution">
  There is no setting that lets the assistant skip confirmation. This is deliberate. A freight job, an invoice or a
  ledger entry should never be created because a sentence was misread.
</Callout>

## Reading vendor invoices

Upload a vendor's PDF invoice in the chat and ask the assistant to propose the vendor cost for a job. It reads the document, extracts the line items, currency and totals, and shows them as a proposed vendor cost against the job. Check the lines against the paper, confirm, and the cost posts with the job's exchange rate.

## Languages

The assistant understands and replies in **Arabic** and **English**. You can switch mid-conversation or mix both in one sentence. Proposals and generated documents remain in the language of your Routel data; the conversation is in yours.

## Permissions in practice

| You are | You ask | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operations | "Create an invoice for this job" | Refused with an explanation: invoicing belongs to Accounting. |
| Sales | "Show me all overdue invoices" | Shows overdue invoices for your customers only. |
| Accounting | "Record rent for September" | Proposal card, then confirm. |
| Management | "Customer profitability for Q3" | Report runs immediately. |

## What it does not do

- It does not act on its own schedule. It only does something when someone asks.
- It does not send email or messages to customers or vendors.
- It does not change users, roles or settings.
- It does not remember conversations across users; each person's chat is their own.

## Audit

Actions confirmed through the assistant are logged against the person who confirmed them, with the same detail as a manual change. See the audit log on the job or record.
