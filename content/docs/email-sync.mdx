Freight correspondence lives in email: booking confirmations, carrier arrival notices, vendor invoices, a customer asking where their container is. Email Sync connects a mailbox to Routel so that traffic lands against the job it belongs to, instead of sitting in an inbox that only one person can search.

## Connecting a mailbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a provider">
    Zoho Mail or Microsoft 365 (Outlook) are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Sign in with the mailbox's own credentials through the provider's standard authorization screen. Routel never
    asks for or stores the password directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it sync">
    Once connected, new mail is scanned for references it recognizes — a job number, a B/L number, a known
    customer or vendor — and linked automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets linked

- **Correspondence** that mentions a job, booking or B/L number is attached to that job's record, so the history of an email thread travels with the shipment.
- **Carrier updates** — an arrival notice, a delay notice — are linked to the job they're about, so they sit with the shipment instead of only existing as an email someone has to remember to check.
- **Attachments** on linked mail feed the [document review queue](/docs/documents): incoming files get classified and matched to a job for a person to approve.

This is distinct from the dedicated finance mailbox used for payment matching — see [Finance (Receivables & Payables)](/docs/finance-receivables).

## Through the assistant

Once a mailbox is connected, "Has the carrier sent an arrival notice for JOB-2026-0412?" or "Show me the last email from this vendor" can be answered from the assistant instead of searching the mailbox directly.

<Callout kind="permission">
  **Admin** connects and manages mailbox sync. Linked mail is visible to whoever can already open the job it is
  attached to.
</Callout>
