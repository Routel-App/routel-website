## Why Routel

- **One flow from quote to cash.** Quotation → booking → charges → invoice → receipt. Every step fills the next, so nothing is typed twice.
- **Profit on every shipment.** Sales and vendor costs sit together on the booking, so you see the margin before you invoice.
- **AI that does the work.** Ask in plain Arabic or English, and it creates, reads, finds and reports. It proposes, you confirm.
- **Nothing slips.** Alerts, labels, tasks and follow-ups keep every shipment and every payment in view.
- **Everyone sees their part.** Roles give each team exactly the modules it needs.
- **Built for the region.** Bilingual ZATCA tax invoices with QR code, local tax rules per country, base currency plus USD.

## Never miss a thing

### Alerts center
One page, five lists, refreshed throughout the day:
- **Arriving soon**: shipments with an ETA in the next 7 days.
- **Payment overdue**: invoices past their due date.
- **Unassigned**: bookings with no operations staff.
- **Missing B/L**: bookings past SI submission with no B/L number.
- **Follow-ups due**: everything promised for today or earlier.

Counters on the sidebar show what is waiting: overdue invoices, documents to review, payments to record, new registrations.

<Shot name="alerts" />

### Labeling Center
Notes and follow-ups across all operations in one place. Put a label on any booking or quotation, give it a due date and an owner, and it shows up in the Labeling Center with today's and overdue counts. Nothing promised to a customer is forgotten.

### Tasks and follow-ups
Every booking has its own tasks and follow-ups, open or done, so the team knows what is left on each shipment.

### The day's brief
Each morning the assistant shows a **Today** card, tailored to your role:
- your open tasks, follow-ups due and arrivals this week;
- bookings past SI with no master B/L, or with no operations staff;
- overdue invoices, drafts left for a week, and vendor bills due this week or past due;
- **delivered shipments whose charges were never invoiced**, the money that quietly gets lost;
- quotations about to expire.

Tap any line and the assistant takes it from there.

## Roles and permissions

Each person sees only what their job needs, and the server enforces the same rules (even for the assistant).

| Role | Works in |
|---|---|
| Admin | Everything, including company settings and users |
| Management | Everything except company settings; can grant credit |
| Accounting | Invoices, receivables and payables, credit and debit notes, expenses, reports |
| Operations | Bookings, documents, bills of lading, vendor costs |
| Sales | Quotations, customers, credit applications, bookings |
| Pricing | Quotations and route rates |

- Add a user with a name, an email and a role. Only the people you list can sign in, or you can allow your whole company domain.
- Money stays with finance: profit, overdue amounts and bank details are hidden from roles that don't need them.
- Credit terms and credit limits can only be granted by admin or management.
- **People on every record:** sales person, operations staff, and who invoiced, issued, prepared or recorded each document, kept as they were at the time.
- **Full history:** every change on a booking is logged field by field (who, what, when), with a status timeline and numbered booking-confirmation revisions.


## Modules

### Daily operations
- **Dashboard**: revenue and outstanding per currency, gross profit, open bookings, pending invoices and recent activity.
- **Bookings**: a list view and a tracking pipeline by status; change the status of many bookings at once.
- **Booking page**: everything about a shipment on one page: shipment details and timeline, charges (sell and cost), profit, documents, labels, tasks, follow-ups and the full activity log.
- **Booking Confirmation**: issue it to the customer, with numbered revisions and a flag when the booking changed since the last issue.
- **Bill of Lading**: create, edit and print House B/Ls.
- **Quotations**: routes and charges, a printable quote, and one-click conversion to a booking.
- **Route Rates**: your rate sheet, with bulk import from Excel or CSV and route analytics.

<Shot name="shipment" />

### Partners
- **Business Partners**: one registry for customers, vendors and agents. Each has contacts, shipments, invoices, ledgers in both currencies, receipts, payments, credit, analytics and activity.
- **Statements**: customer and vendor statements of account, printable and as PDF.
- **Credit control**: payment terms and credit limits per customer, a warning (or a block) when a limit is exceeded, and a public credit application with an approval flow.
- **Self-registration**: a public form where customers and vendors register themselves, the same form your team uses inside the app.

### Finance
- **Invoices**: created from the booking's charges, then finalized, printed in two print styles, and settled in part or in full. Overdue badges and bulk actions.
- **Credit & Debit Notes**: adjust what a customer owes after an invoice is issued, and apply notes to invoices.
- **Receivables & Payables**: receipt vouchers, payment vouchers, vendor bills due, payment runs and remittance slips.
- **Finance Inbox**: payments spotted in your finance mailbox (your invoice numbers in incoming mail, vendor references in outgoing mail), each waiting for one click to record.
- **Expenses**: operating costs outside shipments (rent, salaries, utilities) by category.

### Files and email
- **Documents**: attachments from your mailbox, sorted by the AI and queued for approval. Approving one creates or updates a booking, or makes a draft invoice with the vendor cost.
- **Email Archive**: your synced mailbox, emails linked to bookings, and sending email from inside the app.

### Reports
- **Financial statements:** Profit & Loss, Cash Position, Trial Balance, General Ledger.
- **Receivables:** AR Aging, customer profitability.
- **Payables:** AP Aging, vendor outstanding.
- **Tax:** tax summary under your country's tax name (VAT, SST, PPN…).
- **Partners:** network partners and agent relationships.
- **Exports:** CSV exports of customers, bookings and vendor costs. Aging reports, the general ledger and statements also come as PDF.

### Multi-branch groups
- Each branch keeps its own books, currency and tax.
- One sign-in works across the whole group, with a branch switcher and a consolidated group dashboard.


## The AI assistant

Open it from any page (or **Ctrl/⌘ + G**) and talk to it like a colleague, in Arabic, English or both.

<Shot name="assistant" />

**Runs the whole workflow by chat**
- Quotations, converting them to bookings, and booking charges.
- Draft invoices, finalizing them, receipts, vendor payments and credit notes.
- House B/Ls, tasks, follow-ups and notes.
- Business partners, route rates, expenses and vendor costs.
- Every change appears as a card you can edit. Nothing is saved until you confirm, and the assistant follows the same roles and checks as the app.

**Reads your documents**
- PDFs, images and spreadsheets dropped into the chat.
- Carrier booking confirmations and bills of lading, which fill the booking for you.
- Vendor invoices, turned into cost lines on the booking.
- Company registration certificates (CR, SSM, NIB…), mapped into a new business partner.
- Whole rate sheets, imported as route rates.

<Shot name="documents" />

**Finds and reports**
- Customers, vendors, bookings, invoices and documents.
- Balances, statements, overdue invoices, aging, profit and loss, trial balance, tax summary, profitability and cash position, as an answer or as a file to download.

**Sets up a new company**
- It welcomes the admin and walks through setup one topic at a time, with choices as buttons.
- It covers the company name and address in Arabic and English, tax and bank details, the logo, the team and their roles, charge types and expense categories.
- Upload a registration certificate or a business card and it fills in what it finds.

**Knows where you are**
- The assistant sees the page you have open, so "this booking" or "this invoice" just works.

**Remembers you**
- It keeps your preferences ("always answer in Arabic", "our usual trucker is…").
- A preference is never used to move money or bend a rule, and it never stores passwords or card numbers.

**Answers "how do I…?"**
- A built-in guide explains every page, every step and every rule, including "why was this refused?".

**Works in the background**
- Every synced email attachment is classified (B/L, booking request, booking confirmation, vendor invoice), matched to a booking and turned into a proposal in the Documents queue.

## Built for compliance and control

- **Saudi Arabia:**
  - Bilingual tax invoice (Arabic/English) with the ZATCA phase 1 QR code: seller, VAT number, time, total and VAT.
  - Simplified or standard tax invoice, chosen from the buyer's VAT number.
  - SAR totals on USD invoices.
- **Country rules:** tax name and rates for Saudi Arabia (VAT 15%), Malaysia (SST), Indonesia (PPN), Singapore (GST), and Thailand, Vietnam and the Philippines (VAT).
- **Currencies:** your base currency plus USD, with the exchange rate recorded on every document.
- **Numbering your way:**
  - Your own prefix and branch code.
  - Sequential or time-based numbering.
  - A set starting number when you move from another system.
  - Numbers never repeat or go back.
- **Your data is safe:** each company has its own database. Nightly backups go to cloud storage (the last 14 kept), with a daily storage check and a system-health page.
