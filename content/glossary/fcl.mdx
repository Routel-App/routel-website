---
term: FCL (Full Container Load)
description: A shipment that fills — or books — an entire container by itself, rather than sharing space with other cargo.
---

## What is FCL?

FCL means one shipper's cargo occupies an entire container, sealed at origin and opened only at destination. The alternative is [LCL](/glossary/lcl), where several shippers' cargo shares one container.

## Key points

- The container is loaded, sealed and released as a single unit — nobody else's cargo rides inside it.
- Pricing is per container (by size and type — 20GP, 40GP, 40HC), not per cubic metre or kilogram.
- Because the seal stays intact from origin to destination, FCL generally means fewer handling touches and less risk of damage or loss than LCL.
- A single [Bill of Lading](/glossary/bill-of-lading) typically covers the whole container.

## Example scenario

A furniture exporter has enough stock to fill a 40-foot high-cube container for one buyer. Booking it as FCL means the container goes straight from the factory to the port, onto the vessel, and to the buyer's warehouse without being opened or repacked along the way.
